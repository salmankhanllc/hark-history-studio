Why This Isn't a Generic History Video

  • One locked look. Period colour, film grain and lighting rules are written into every single prompt — clips match each other.
  • Firing → impact, always. A gun firing is followed immediately by its destruction. No filler, no slow scenic shots.
  • Real sound only. Each scene carries a combat sound cue synced to the picture — no narration, no music bed.
  • Archive first. Real archival footage from free sources, enhanced with the studio recipe, beats fake-looking AI footage. AI generation is the fallback, labelled as generated.
  • The locked title format. Three title lines in the top black bar only — L1 at 0s, L2 (gold) at ~7s, L3 typewriter at ~15.5s. Titles never touch the footage.

How It Works

  1. Load the styleBattle of Britain template above — era, palette, grain and format.
  2. Create the breakdownTopic, hook and date in Create. Scenes are written style-locked, firing → impact, aftermath last.
  3. Find or generate each clipUse the 9 free archive links per scene, or generate with the free SANA model / your Fal key.
  4. Enhance & assembleFollow the 8-step recipe per scene, check the Reel Preview and Quality Checklist, then export the Production Sheet.
